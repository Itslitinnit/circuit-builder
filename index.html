Quick Start

  1. Drag any component from the left panel onto the canvas
  2. Click the Wire button, then click two component pins to connect them
  3. Press Esc to cancel drawing a wire
  4. Press Ctrl + Z to undo your last action
  5. Click Select before exporting as PNG
  6. Hold Spacebar + drag to pan the canvas
  7. Click a component to rotate or rename it
